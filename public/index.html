About

Asteroid Watch shows which space rocks are flying past Earth, how close they get, and whether any of them is a threat.

Every dot is a real asteroid on its real orbit around the Sun, from NASA/JPL's live data (refreshed every 10 minutes). Drag the timeline to move through time. Pick an asteroid to see its size next to something familiar, then Watch the flyby to see its path past Earth and the Moon.

Reading the numbers

  • LD is a lunar distance: 384,400 km, the average distance to the Moon.
  • Sizes are usually estimated from brightness, so they're given as a range.
  • The solar-system view uses a two-body orbit model, accurate to within a few hours near an approach. The flyby view uses JPL Horizons, which includes Earth's and the Moon's pull.
